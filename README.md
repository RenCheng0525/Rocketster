# Rocketster 火箭跑車

🙌 **A tribute to the Tesla Roadster, the car that flies.** Unveiled in October 2026, it climbed a curved ramp, hung upside down on suction fans and flipped through the air on cold-gas thrusters. This game is our love letter to that moment.

Live Demo: [https://rencheng0525.github.io/Rocketster/](https://rencheng0525.github.io/Rocketster/)

Generated by Claude with prompts only, no manual code editing.

> 🚀 A hand-flown rocket car. Charge up a towering C-shaped ramp, stick to it upside down with a suction fan, shoot off the lip and fire cold-gas thrusters by hand to land wheels-down. Inspired by real-world thruster-car demos, set at a night-time test site.

## Overview

A web-based **3D rocket-car flight game** in a single HTML file. You drive up a 24 m inverted ramp, hang upside down at the top, and launch into the air, where every thruster is yours to fire: lift jet, main thrust, pitch and roll. Or skip the ramp, lift straight off the landing pad and pull a barrel roll. The scene is rendered with **three.js**, and all sound is synthesized in real time. There are no image or audio files.

## Features

### 🛣️ Two Ways to Fly

- **C-Ramp**: Throttle down the runway, climb a ramp that curves up a vertical wall and over the top, hang upside down on the lip, then drive off into the air.
- **Hover Flip**: Start on the landing pad. Jet straight up, roll a full turn (or two, or a backflip), then set it back down.
- **Hop Anytime**: Press the lift jet on the ground to take off on the spot. In C-Ramp mode you can hop, land, and keep driving.
- **Choose in the Menu**: Both modes sit side by side on the start screen, with a short description of each.

### 🌀 Fully Manual Flight

- **No Assists**: You work the throttle, switch on the suction fan, and fire every thruster yourself.
- **Suction Fan**: Pulls the car onto the track with up to 2 g. It needs a moment to spin up and drains the battery, so switch it on before the ramp.
- **The Upside-Down Lip**: The top of the ramp is a flat, inverted section with no centrifugal force at all. Only the fan keeps you there. Stop for 1.5 seconds for a bonus.
- **Lift Jet Points at the Roof**: It always pushes toward the roof. Fire it while upside down and it drives you into the ground.
- **About Four Seconds of Gas**: The cold-gas tank is small. Spend too much on the way up and there's nothing left to brake with.
- **Attitude Indicators**: Two circles at the top right show the car from the side and from behind. Both turn green when the wheels face the ground.
- **G-Meter**: Next to altitude and vertical speed, a G-force reading shows what someone in the seat would feel: 1 G parked, close to 0 G in free fall, and around 6 G hitting the bottom of the ramp at speed. It turns orange at 5 G and red at 9 G.
- **Landing Rules**: A touchdown counts only if the wheels are down, the sink rate is 5.5 m/s or less, and the car isn't spinning.

### 🌃 Night Test Site

- **Glowing Ramp**: A white ramp with red LED edges and lane lines, hung from a steel truss tower with red warning lights on top.
- **Runway**: A red LED line along the runway, small white edge lights and orange marker posts, like the real launch event.
- **Atmosphere**: A starry sky, a distant city skyline with lit windows, and searchlight beams slowly sweeping the sky.
- **Floodlights**: Ground lamps light the ramp from below.
- **Neon Landing Pad**: A pulsing cyan frame marks where to land.
- **Cold-Gas Plumes**: Every thruster puffs white vapor in the direction it fires.
- **Bloom**: Lights, LEDs and plumes glow softly.
- **Original Car**: A low, white two-seater with full-width light bars, designed for this game.

### 🎥 Camera

- **Two Views**: Switch between **Spectator** view and **Driver** view with the 🎥 button in the key log panel, or `V`. Your choice is remembered.
- **Spectator View**: A chase camera that follows the car from the side.
- **Driver View**: You sit in the driver’s seat and the camera pitches and rolls with the car. Climbing the wall you stare at the sky, hanging on the lip the world is upside down, and in a barrel roll the horizon spins around you.
- **Nothing in the Way**: In Driver view the car body isn’t drawn, so the view ahead is completely clear. Its shadow still falls on the ground, so you can judge your height. Thruster vapor near your head fades out instead of whiting out the screen.
- **Drag to Look**: In Spectator view, drag with the mouse or a finger to swing the camera around the car. In Driver view, dragging turns your head to look around.
- **Zoom**: Scroll the wheel, or pinch with two fingers. In Driver view this widens or narrows the field of view.
- **Reset**: Double-click, double-tap or press `C` to return to the default view.
- **Looks Ahead**: The car sits left of center, so the road and ramp ahead stay in view on the right.
- **Frames the Ramp**: The camera pulls back automatically near the ramp so the whole structure fits on screen.

### 🎵 Sound

- **Electric Motor**: A whine that rises with speed and swells when you throttle.
- **Road and Wind**: Tyre roar on the ground and wind rush in the air, both scaling with speed.
- **Fan**: A click as it switches on, then a rising whirr as it spins up.
- **Thrusters**: A deep hiss and roar for the lift jet and main thrust, sharp short puffs for the pitch and roll thrusters.
- **Event Sounds**: Launch whoosh, a chime for the upside-down stop, warning beeps, a touchdown thump with a rising chime, and a crash boom.
- **One Sound Button**: 🔊 in the key log panel, or `M`, mutes and unmutes everything.

### 🤖 AI Demo

- **Flies by Keys Only**: Up to 40 times a second, the AI reads the instruments and decides which keys to hold. It never moves the car directly.
- **Live Key Log**: Every press appears in a side panel with the time, who pressed it (you or the AI), what it did, and how long it was held. A row of keycaps lights up while keys are held.
- **New Personality Every Run**: In C-Ramp mode it rolls a personality: *Steady*, *Speedster*, *Easygoing* or *Show-off*. Each one randomizes cruise speed, when to switch on the fan, whether to stop upside down, exit speed, roll direction, reaction time and whether to show off with a hover.
- **New Trick Every Run**: In Hover Flip mode it picks a single roll, a double roll, a backflip, or a roll followed by a hover, turning left or right.
- **Not Perfect**: Now and then it gets "distracted" and skips a decision, so no two runs look the same.
- **Hand Over Anytime**: Press `I` or the AI button mid-flight to switch between you and the AI.

### 📊 Scoring System

| Points | Event |
|--------|-------|
| 1000 | Safe landing |
| up to 800 | Soft touchdown (the slower your sink rate, the more points) |
| 600 | Landing on the pad |
| 4 per % | Cold gas left |
| 300 | Stopping upside down on the lip for 1.5 seconds |
| 500 | Each full rotation in the air |

Grades: **S** at 2900 or more, **A** at 2300, **B** at 1700, **C** below that.

The results screen also shows your **peak G-force** for the run.

### ✨ Small Touches

- **Bilingual**: Traditional Chinese and English, chosen from your browser language and switchable from the start menu or with `L`.
- **Context Hints**: A banner tells you what to do next, such as switching on the fan before the ramp or braking before the ground.
- **Back to Menu**: The results screen offers another try, the AI demo, or a return to the menu to switch modes.
- **Phone Friendly**: Respects notches and home bars, and widens the camera view on portrait screens.
- **Saved Settings**: Language, sound and camera view are remembered in your browser.

## 🛠 Technical Highlights

- **2D Physics, 3D Rendering**: Motion is solved in the vertical plane (position, pitch) plus a roll angle around the car's forward axis. three.js draws it in full 3D.
- **Piecewise Track**: The ramp is a quarter arc, a 6 m vertical wall, a second quarter arc and a 5 m inverted lip. Each piece reports its position, tangent angle and curvature.
- **Grip From Normal Force**: The track pushes back with `N = v²·κ + g·cos θ + suction`. Traction is limited to μ·N, and when N drops below zero the car falls off.
- **Fixed-Step Simulation**: Physics runs at a fixed 240 Hz with an accumulator, independent of frame rate.
- **AI Without Cheating**: For roll and pitch, the AI computes the fastest rotation it can still stop in time and fires thrusters to match it. To land, it checks whether the remaining height is enough to brake at the current sink rate.
- **Simulated Before Shipping**: Physics, landing checks and the AI don't depend on rendering, so they were run headlessly in Node across hundreds of AI flights per mode.
- **Synthesized Audio**: Motor, road, fan, wind and thruster sounds are oscillators and filtered noise from the Web Audio API, controlled every frame by the simulation.
- **G-Force From Velocity**: Every physics step, the meter takes the change in the car’s velocity, adds gravity back in and smooths the result over about 0.1 seconds. Parked reads 1 G, free fall reads 0 G.
- **Invisible Car, Real Shadow**: In Driver view the car’s materials stop drawing to the screen, but the shadow pass still renders them, so the car vanishes from view while its shadow stays on the ground.
- **Instanced Truss**: The steel tower's hundreds of beams are drawn as a single instanced mesh.
- **Text in One Table**: All interface text lives in one Chinese and English dictionary. HTML elements mark what they need with `data-i18n`, so switching language is one function.
- **Single HTML File**: No build step. three.js r128 and its bloom post-processing load from public CDNs (the game falls back to plain rendering if bloom fails to load), and the Chakra Petch font comes from Google Fonts.
